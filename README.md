## 🪷 &nbsp; Ātman · *Who I Am*

> ***karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana***
> *Your right is to the work alone — never to its fruits.* — Bhagavad Gītā 2.47

I'm **Devasish** (aka `TechGen_DM`), a Computer Science & AI student at **Scaler School of Technology** and **BITS Pilani**, Bengaluru. I treat building like a practice, not a performance — show up daily, do the work honestly, let the results take care of themselves.

- 🧘 &nbsp; **Path:** local-first AI — tools that run on your own machine, with nothing leaving your laptop
- ⚙️ &nbsp; **Practice:** automation with n8n, voice agents and LLM pipelines
- 🌐 &nbsp; **Craft:** full-stack products in React, Next.js, TypeScript and Node.js
- 🏁 &nbsp; **Pilgrimages:** hackathons, Kaggle, Hacktoberfest and open source — all built in public
- 🤝 &nbsp; **Open to:** internships, freelance work and hackathon teams
